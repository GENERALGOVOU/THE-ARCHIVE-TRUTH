About this archive

Scope: USA, UK, France, NATO / Five Eyes, 1945–present, plus colonial wars and Cold War regime-change operations. Each card shows years, actors, category and evidentiary status.

How to verify: search national archives (US NARA, UK National Archives), Church Committee reports, Senate hearings, EU STOA ECHELON reports, court verdicts, Snowden/PCLOB reports. No secret claims without a paper trail are listed as proven.
